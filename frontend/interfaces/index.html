Turning Government Challenges into Scalable Innovation

Connect government departments with verified startups to discover, pilot, procure and scale innovative solutions for public challenges.

The GovBridge lifecycle

From Problem to Public Impact

Every listed challenge moves through a transparent, trackable journey — from the department that raised it to the startup that solves it at scale.

How GovBridge works

A structured six-stage pathway so no promising idea gets lost, and no pilot scales without proof.

01

Identify

Government departments publish real-world challenges with clear outcomes and budgets.

02

Discover

Eligible startups browse, filter and submit innovative solutions.

03

Evaluate

Solutions are scored against transparent, weighted criteria by reviewers.

04

Pilot

Selected solutions enter a controlled, time-boxed pilot or proof of concept.

05

Procure

Successful pilots move into a formal procurement pipeline.

06

Scale

Proven solutions expand across departments, regions and states.

Why GovBridge?

Built for how public procurement actually needs to work with early-stage innovation.

Transparent Evaluation

Verified Startup Ecosystem

Pilot Before Scale

Nothing reaches full procurement without a tracked, time-boxed pilot outcome.

Faster Discovery

Data-Driven Decisions

End-to-End Tracking
